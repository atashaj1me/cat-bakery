How discovery works

Cheats unlock through gameplay. Some appear when you answer specific MCQs correctly. Others reveal themselves only after acing a particular phase, completing an essay, or maintaining a streak. There's no menu to grind — play the campaigns and notebooks naturally and the vault fills itself.

Each unlocked cheat shows how you discovered it — a small narrative provenance that ties the trick back to the moment you earned it.
