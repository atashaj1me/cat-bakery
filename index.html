How this game maps to the exam

The ECON201 final is 75 minutes: 15 MCQs (60 pts) and essays (~40 pts). Per the practice exam, post-midterm material is ~80% of MCQs (Ch 14, 18, 19, 20, 21, 22, 23). The essays are guaranteed to be either:

  • Essay 1 — Price-ceiling welfare (Day 4 of the campaign; or Chapter 2 reference)
  • Essay 2 — Tariff surplus table (Day 6 of the campaign; or Chapter 3 reference)

Complete the campaign (or score ≥60% on Chapter 6) to unlock the 75-minute Royal Examination — same shape as the real final.
