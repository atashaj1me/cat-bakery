How this game maps to the exam

The ECON201 final is 75 minutes: 15 MCQs (60 pts) and essays (~40 pts). Per the practice exam, post-midterm material is ~80% of MCQs (Ch 14, 18, 19, 20, 21, 22, 23). The essays are guaranteed to be either:

  • Essay 1 — Price-ceiling welfare (covered in Chapter 2)
  • Essay 2 — Tariff surplus table (covered in Chapter 3)

Each chapter ends with three MCQs lifted directly from the vault's archetypes — so muscle memory transfers straight to exam recall.
